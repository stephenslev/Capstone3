### 📋 Migration Playbook Recommendations

If the project includes a data migration component, the **Migration Playbook** recommends the following:

#### 🔍 Discovery Outputs
The discovery phase should produce **three key outputs**:
1. A **system inventory**
2. A **data classification summary** — noting any regulated or sensitive data categories
3. A **preliminary risk register** — the playbook explicitly warns that engagements skipping a formal risk register during discovery are *"disproportionately likely to encounter unplanned schedule slippage during the cutover phase"*

#### 🛡️ Execution Safeguards
The playbook recommends the following safeguards during execution:
- **Batch-based migration** — rather than a single cutover, to allow validation checkpoints and limit the impact of any single failure
- **Checksum validation** — each batch should be validated against source-system checksums before the next batch begins
- **Per-batch rollback plans** — a rollback plan should exist for every batch, not only for the final cutover
- **Daily status updates** — required during active migration windows, even on days with no notable progress
